1. Welcome to WaitLess

WaitLess replaces the old paper-ticket queue system with a digital one that puts you in control. Instead of sitting in a corridor for hours with no idea when you'll be seen, WaitLess lets you:

  • See your live queue position on your phone at any time
  • Receive WhatsApp messages when your turn is approaching
  • Leave the waiting area and return when it's almost your turn
  • Track your ticket from registration through to completion
  • Book appointments online without coming to the hospital
  • Give feedback on your hospital experience
Key benefit: You no longer need to sit in the waiting area for hours. Register, track your position on your phone, and return when you receive a WhatsApp message saying your turn is approaching.

2. Getting Started — Three Ways to Register

Option A: At the Reception Desk

This is the most common way to register, especially for first-time visitors.

1

Go to the reception desk

Tell the receptionist you need to see a doctor. They will search for your existing record or create a new one.

2

Provide your details

The receptionist will capture: your full name, national ID, phone number, address, date of birth, gender, next of kin (name and phone), what's wrong (chief complaint), and which department you need.

3

Give WhatsApp consent

If you have WhatsApp on your phone, tell the receptionist yes — you'll receive queue updates automatically.

4

Receive your ticket

You'll get a printed ticket with a code like OPD-205 and a QR code. You can also have it shared to your phone.

Option B: Self-Registration from Your Phone

If you have a smartphone, you can register before you even arrive at the hospital.

1

Open the hospital app

Visit the WaitLess app URL on your phone browser. Tap "Get started today".

2

Fill in your details

Enter your name, phone number, and select the department you need. Describe what's wrong.

3

Get your ticket number

You'll receive a digital ticket number immediately. When you arrive at the hospital, go straight to triage — no need to queue at reception.

Option C: Kiosk Check-In at the Hospital Entrance

A self-service kiosk tablet is available at the hospital entrance for returning patients.

1

Scan your QR code or enter your phone number

Point your patient QR code at the kiosk camera, or type in your phone number.

2

Select department and reason

Choose which department you need and briefly describe your reason for visiting.

3

Get your ticket

The kiosk displays your ticket number. Proceed to the waiting area.

3. Tracking Your Queue Position

Once you have your ticket code (e.g., OPD-205), you can track your position in the queue at any time from your phone.

1

Open the tracking page

Visit the tracking page (the URL is printed on your ticket, or scan the QR code on your ticket with your phone camera).

2

Enter your ticket code

Type in your ticket code, e.g., OPD-205. The page updates in real time — no need to refresh.

3

See your live status

You will see:

  • Current position — how many patients are ahead of you
  • Estimated wait time — how long until you're called
  • Current stage — Registered, Triage, Waiting, Called, or Served
  • Department directions — where to go next
  • Notification history — messages sent to you
Did you know? The tracking page works on any phone with a browser — no app download needed. It even works on slow internet connections.

4. WhatsApp Notifications

If you gave consent during registration and provided your WhatsApp number, you will automatically receive these messages:

When Message you receive
At registration "You are registered. Your ticket is OPD-205. Track your queue at [URL]"
2-3 patients before your turn "Your turn is approaching. Please return to the OPD waiting area."
When you are called "OPD-205, please proceed to Room 3."
If you miss your turn "You missed your turn. Please see the reception desk to be recalled."
If you are transferred "You have been moved to the Laboratory queue. Your new ticket is LAB-206."
Prescription ready "Your prescription is ready at the Pharmacy window."
Lab results ready "Your lab results are ready. Please return to the doctor."
Important: Make sure your phone number is correct during registration. If you don't have WhatsApp, you can still track your queue position using the tracking page.

5. Triage Priority System

After registration, a triage nurse will assess your condition and assign a priority level. This determines the order in which patients are seen — it is not first-come, first-served. Patients with more urgent conditions are seen first.

RED — Resuscitation Life-threatening. Seen immediately, ahead of everyone else.
YELLOW — Urgent Should be seen within 60 minutes. High priority.
GREEN — Standard Routine cases. Seen in order of arrival within this group.
BLACK — Deceased No further treatment possible.
If you feel your condition is getting worse while waiting, tell the triage nurse immediately. The system also automatically alerts nurses if Red or Yellow patients have been waiting too long.

Your Phone — Tracking Page

On your phone, you see your personal queue status with position, wait time, and directions to your next department.

7. Booking an Appointment

You can book an appointment in advance through the patient portal on your phone.

1

Log in to the patient portal

Open the WaitLess app and log in with your username and password. If you don't have an account, tap "Register" to create one.

2

Go to "Book Appointment"

Tap the "Book Appointment" tab in the patient portal menu.

3

Select date and service

Choose a date and the type of service you need. Available time slots will appear.

4

Book your slot

Tap an available slot and confirm. You'll receive a reminder before your appointment.

Need to cancel? Go to "Book Appointment" in the portal, find your appointment, and tap "Cancel".

8. Leaving and Returning

One of the biggest benefits of WaitLess is that you do not need to sit in the waiting area the entire time.

1

Check your position on your phone

Open the tracking page and see how many patients are ahead of you.

2

Step out if you have time

If there are several patients ahead, you can get fresh air, use the restroom, or get something to eat.

3

Return when you get the WhatsApp message

You'll receive a "Your turn is approaching" message 2-3 patients before your turn. Return to the waiting area promptly.

If you miss your turn: Don't panic. Go to the reception desk and let them know. You will be recalled and reinserted into the queue fairly.

9. Giving Feedback

After your visit is complete, you can rate your experience to help the hospital improve.

1

Log in to the patient portal

Open the WaitLess app and log in.

2

Go to "Feedback"

Tap the "Feedback" tab. You'll see your completed visits that are ready for feedback.

3

Rate your experience

Rate: overall satisfaction, cleanliness, communication, and wait time. Submit your feedback.

10. Changing Language

WaitLess supports three languages. You can switch at any time:

1

Tap the language selector

Look for the language button in the top navigation bar.

2

Choose your language

Select from: English, chiShona, or isiNdebele.

3

The entire interface switches

All labels, instructions, and messages will now appear in your chosen language.

11. Troubleshooting

I can't find the tracking page

The URL is printed on your ticket. You can also scan the QR code on your ticket with your phone camera, which will open the tracking page automatically.

I'm not receiving WhatsApp messages

  • Make sure you gave consent during registration
  • Check that your phone number was entered correctly
  • Ensure you have an active WhatsApp account on that number
  • If the hospital internet is down, messages will be sent when it comes back

I missed my turn — what do I do?

Go to the reception desk and tell them your ticket number. They will recall you and put you back in the queue fairly.

The tracking page says "Unable to reach the server"

This means the hospital server is temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few moments. The queue board screens in the waiting area will still show your position.

I don't have a smartphone

You can still use WaitLess. The reception desk will give you a printed ticket, and the TV screens in the waiting area show the live queue. If you have WhatsApp on a basic phone, you'll still receive messages.

I need to see a different department

Tell the doctor or nurse. They can transfer you to another department queue (e.g., from OPD to Laboratory). You'll get a new ticket number and a WhatsApp message telling you where to go.
